## 3. What we added to make it more beautiful

- **A new hero video showing all nine shapes.** The previous hero video had no scope to add more shapes, so we generated a new hero video to showcase all nine diamonds. Finalising the hero video alone took a full day to meet your requirements, and we put our best effort into both image generation and video generation to give you the best possible result. That is why this revision took slightly more time to complete.
- **See the difference, rebuilt for clarity.** Your two diagrams were enhanced to a large, sharp size. The Centurion 100 now wipes over the traditional 57 as the count runs from 57 to 100, then the two sit side by side.
- **The Diamond Journey.** One stone moves through four stages (rough, sawn, shaped, finished) while a ring of 100 ticks lights up as you scroll. The stage images are generated illustrations, so the caption *"An illustrative study of rough to polished form"* stays.
- **Seamless flow.** Colour and light match at every section hand-off, so visitors never feel a new-section line.
- **Phone-ready.** A dedicated vertical hero, checked at nine screen sizes, with a clean static version for visitors who turn motion off.
